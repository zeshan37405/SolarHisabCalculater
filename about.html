SOLAR HISAB CALCULATOR

About

Solar Hisab Calculator is a free, independent educational tool designed to help households make an initial estimate of electricity use, solar array capacity, panel count and broad project budget.

How the estimate works

The calculator adds the energy used by the appliances entered, applies a simplified peak-sun-hours assumption and system-loss factor, and estimates the number of reference 580-watt panels. The safety-margin option adds 20% to the calculated energy requirement.

Cost information

Country cost figures are illustrative planning assumptions. They are not live prices, supplier quotations or verified current market averages. They can differ substantially from actual local offers.

Independent project

This project is not affiliated with a solar panel, inverter, installer or electricity provider. No product endorsement is implied.

Important limitation

The tool does not inspect a roof, calculate shading, design battery storage, verify electrical protection, check local regulations or guarantee savings. A qualified local solar professional must review any real installation.
