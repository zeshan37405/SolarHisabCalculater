Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: November 17, 2026.

Information entered into the calculator

Appliance names, wattage, usage hours and country selection are processed in your browser to display an estimate. The calculator does not require an account and the project code does not intentionally transmit these entries to a project-owned server.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted as a static site. The hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser details, requested pages and security logs under its own policies.

Cookies and tracking

The project does not intentionally add advertising cookies or third-party analytics in this version. Browser settings and hosting-provider infrastructure may still affect technical requests.

External links

Links to external websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are controlled by those websites, not by this project.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website changes. For questions or corrections, use the issue or discussion features on the public project repository.
