Frequently asked questions

Is this solar estimate guaranteed?

No. It is a preliminary estimate based on your inputs and simplified assumptions. A site survey and electrical design are essential.

Does the calculator include batteries?

No. Battery sizing depends on backup loads, required hours, battery chemistry, usable capacity and inverter compatibility. Ask a qualified installer to design storage separately.

Why can my actual quote differ?

Prices depend on equipment brands, taxes, labor, roof structure, mounting, wiring, permits, grid connection and local market conditions.

Can I use this outside one country?

Yes. Select one of the listed markets. Country cost figures are broad planning assumptions, not live supplier prices.

Does the website store my appliance information?

The calculator processes your entries in your browser. It does not require an account or send appliance entries to a project server.
