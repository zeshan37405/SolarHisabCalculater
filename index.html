Frequently asked questions

Is this solar estimate guaranteed?

No. It is a preliminary estimate based on the values entered and simplified assumptions. A site survey and electrical design are essential.

Does the calculator include batteries?

The current version estimates energy and a broad system budget. Battery sizing is not included because backup duration, battery chemistry and usable capacity must be selected separately.

Why does my actual quote differ?

Prices depend on equipment brands, taxes, labor, roof structure, mounting, wiring, permits, grid connection and local market conditions.

Can I use this outside one country?

Yes. Select one of the available markets. The country cost figures are broad planning assumptions, not live supplier prices.
